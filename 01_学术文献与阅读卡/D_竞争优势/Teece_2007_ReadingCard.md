## 2. 主要理论

动态能力涉及感知机会、把握机会和重构资源，重点在企业如何适应变化，而不只是把既有流程执行得更快。

### Key Theory & Findings
Teece operationalises the concept of **Dynamic Capabilities** by breaking it down into distinct microfoundations that allow business enterprises to sustain superior performance in fast-changing environments:
1. **Sensing (and Shaping)**: The analytical scanning, search, and exploration processes to identify customer needs, technological changes, and latent market shifts (pp. 1322–1326).
2. **Seizing**: The mobilization of resources to capture value from identified opportunities through appropriate business model design, product architecture, and boundary decisions (pp. 1326–1334).
3. **Transforming (Reconfiguring)**: The continuous alignment and realignment of specific tangible and intangible assets, organizational routines, and governance mechanisms to escape path dependency and asset stagnation (pp. 1334–1341).

### Value for UNIQLO vs ZARA Comparison
This paper provides the indispensable dynamic framework connecting internal capabilities to real-world market turbulence in the Chinese retail landscape:
- **Evaluating ZARA's Sensing and Seizing**: ZARA has historically mastered rapid operational sensing through store managers' daily digital feedback logs, POS terminal data aggregation, and rapid prototyping in Arteixo. However, ZARA struggles with *strategic transforming* in China: its rigid European production footprint and escalating price tags have hindered it from adapting to post-pandemic consumer pragmatism, leading to massive store closures across lower-tier cities.
- **Diagnosing UNIQLO's Dynamic Transformation Deficit**: UNIQLO possesses immense scale and operational prowess, yet its highly centralized, standardized merchandising routines led to sluggish sensing of regional micro-climates in Greater China (e.g. over-stocking heavy winter outerwear in mild southern winters, resulting in FY2024–FY2025 operating profit contraction).
- **Justifying the Recommendation for UNIQLO**: The proposed recommendation (scenario-driven regional merchandising and localized digital styling advisory) directly addresses UNIQLO's need to institutionalize localized *sensing* and asset *reconfiguration*. It allows UNIQLO to leverage its existing massive store network while introducing regional flexibility, transforming static scale into dynamic market responsiveness.

### Research Limitations
- The framework operates at a relatively high level of abstraction, making empirical operationalization of specific microfoundations challenging in marketing practice.
- Does not offer explicit quantitative guidelines on the financial cost-benefit threshold for dynamic realignment versus continuing standardized mass routines (p. 1344).
- Primarily developed from high-tech and industrial settings; requires adaptation to retail supply chains and omni-channel consumer engagement.
