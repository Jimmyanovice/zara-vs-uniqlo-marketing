## Part 1: 4Ps Marketing Strategy Comparison Table
| Dimension | UNIQLO | ZARA | Theoretical Foundation |
|-----------|--------|------|------------------------|
| **Product** | Product specialist path<br>• Narrow SKU, long lifecycle (6–12 months)<br>• Core focus on functional fabrics & universal basic wear<br>• Covers all age groups and daily scenarios | Differentiation-led fast fashion path<br>• Massive SKU, short lifecycle (2–4 weeks)<br>• Core focus on runway-inspired trendy designs<br>• Targets young, fashion-focused consumers | Big Middle hypothesis: product specialization vs market generalization <br> *(Azuma et al., 2022)* |
| **Price** | EDLP-like pattern<br>• Stable daily prices<br>• Infrequent, scheduled seasonal promotions<br>• Builds long-term price-value trust | Hi-Lo pricing pattern<br>• Full price for new collections<br>• 50–70% off end-of-season clearance<br>• Price discrimination by willingness to pay | Two dominant retail pricing models: EDLP vs Hi-Lo <br> *(Anderson & Fox, 2019)* |
| **Place** | Highly integrated omnichannel model<br>• Unified pricing, inventory & membership online-offline<br>• Wide coverage in residential & transport hub locations<br>• Supports in-store pickup/return for online orders | Store-centric omnichannel model<br>• Core brand touchpoints: prime-location flagship stores<br>• Online channel as supplementary sales<br>• Limited cross-channel service integration | Omnichannel sensory coherence theory; store convenience as necessary patronage condition <br> *(Azuma et al., 2022; Cenizo, 2026)* |
| **Promotion** | Functional value-oriented communication<br>• Highlights fabric technology and practicality<br>• Designer collaborations for product appeal<br>• Content: styling guides & product tutorials | Emotional fashion-oriented communication<br>• Highlights fashion identity and trend leadership<br>• Editorial-style visual campaigns<br>• Content: aesthetic lookbooks & lifestyle scenarios | Digital sensory brand experience theory <br> *(Cenizo, 2026)* |
